## Conversion

- The G-code is split by its `; FEATURE: <type>` comments, and only moves that travel in X/Y/Z *and* advance E are summed; pure E moves (retract, unretract, purge) belong to no type, as in the GUI's GCodeProcessor.
- Weight = length × π × (diameter / 2)² × density, with diameter and density read from the sliced 3MF's `Metadata/project_settings.config` (`filament_diameter`, `filament_density`).
- The G-code has a `Floating vertical shell` type that the GUI "Line type" legend does not show. At 11% it is 12.84 g; added to the 18.40 g of `Internal solid infill` it is exactly the GUI's internal solid infill of 31.24 g, so the tool merges it into internal solid infill (every other density matches as well).
- The per-type lengths add up exactly to the G-code header's `total filament length`.
